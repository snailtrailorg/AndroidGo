Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

AndroidGo — Last updated: 2026-06-11

1. Data Collection / 数据收集

AndroidGo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

AndroidGo 不收集、存储或传输任何个人数据。本应用完全在您的设备上离线运行。

2. Local Storage / 本地存储

Game records (SGF files) are saved locally in the app's private storage directory. They are never uploaded or shared.

棋谱文件（SGF 格式）保存在应用私有存储目录中，不会上传或分享。

3. Network Access / 网络访问

AndroidGo does not use the internet. No network permissions are requested.

AndroidGo 不使用互联网，不请求任何网络权限。

4. Open Source Components / 开源组件

AndroidGo includes open source software. See the About screen in the app for license details.

AndroidGo 使用了开源软件组件，协议详情见应用内"关于"页面。
